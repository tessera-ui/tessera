## [v0.1.0] - 2026-11-16

### Added

- First release. Provides design-system-independent building blocks for
  Tessera components: alignment and shape definitions, gesture recognizers,
  scroll physics, and the `ModifierExt` layout, window inset, semantics,
  interaction, and visual modifiers (`padding`, `size`, `fill_*`, `align`,
  `semantics`, `alpha`, `clip_shape`, and more).
